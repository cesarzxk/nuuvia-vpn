Mesa3D software OpenGL (llvmpipe)
=================================

Nuuvia bundles an unmodified Mesa3D build as a last-resort OpenGL renderer
for machines without a working hardware OpenGL/DX12 driver.

- Version: 26.3.0
- Build: mesa3d-26.3.0-release-msvc (x64), from
  https://github.com/pal1000/mesa-dist-win/releases/tag/26.3.0
- Upstream source: https://gitlab.freedesktop.org/mesa/mesa/-/tree/mesa-26.3.0
- Files: opengl32.dll, libgallium_wgl.dll

The DLLs are distributed unmodified. They are not signed with the
Nuuvia/SignPath certificate.
